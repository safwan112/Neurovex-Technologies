The purchase price is only one part of a printer's cost. To choose between buying and leasing, begin with how your team actually prints.

## Measure volume and requirements

Count monthly pages, color jobs, required paper sizes, and scanning needs. An oversized machine ties up budget; an undersized one slows work.

## Compare total cost

Include supplies, maintenance, service visits, and electricity in the purchase or lease calculation. Check exactly what each agreement covers.

## When buying makes sense

Buying may suit a stable print volume when the company can manage supplies and repairs. It also gives you more freedom over how long the equipment stays in use.

## When leasing makes sense

Leasing can spread costs and simplify equipment renewal. Review included print volumes, service terms, and the length of the commitment.
