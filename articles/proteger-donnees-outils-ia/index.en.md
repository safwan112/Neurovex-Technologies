Pasting a contract or customer file into an AI assistant seems harmless. Yet depending on the tool and its settings, that information may be stored or used to train models.

## Prefer business plans

Enterprise versions of AI tools usually offer contractual guarantees: data not used for training, limited retention, and central administration. Read these terms before choosing.

## Classify information

Define what can be shared: public content, internal documents, sensitive data. Customer personal data, financial information, and passwords should never be entered into an unapproved tool.

## Manage access

Use business accounts tied to the company directory, with strong authentication. When an employee leaves, their access and history must be removable.

## Train your teams

Most leaks come from well-intentioned use. A few concrete examples of what is and isn't allowed work better than a long procedure.
