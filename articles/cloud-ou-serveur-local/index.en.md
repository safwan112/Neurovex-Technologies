Where should your company store files and run its applications? The answer depends less on technology than on your business, your connection, and your internal resources.

## Cloud strengths

The cloud gives access to tools from anywhere, with no hardware to maintain on site. The provider handles updates and part of the security. The cost is monthly and scales with the number of users.

## On-premises strengths

An on-site server remains relevant for older business applications, large file volumes, or an unreliable internet connection. It does require maintenance, backups, and planned replacement.

## The hybrid option

Many SMEs combine both: email and office tools in the cloud, heavy files or specific software on a local server, with an off-site backup.

## Questions to ask

Is your connection reliable and backed up? Who handles maintenance? Which data must stay in Morocco? What is the total cost over five years?
