Generative AI is no longer reserved for large companies. Accessible tools already help SMEs save time on everyday tasks, as long as they pick the right use cases.

## Write faster

First drafts of emails, product sheets, job offers, or posts: AI provides a starting point that your teams review and adjust. The gain comes from skipping the blank page, not from publishing unchecked text.

## Summarize long documents

Meeting notes, contracts, or tenders can be summarized in a few points. Always check key details against the original document.

## Support internal help

A base of answers to frequent questions, enhanced with AI, helps employees quickly find a procedure or a contact.

## Prepare simple analyses

From a sales or ticket spreadsheet, AI can spot trends and suggest charts. It speeds up reading the data without replacing the decision.

## Translate and adapt

For international customers, AI helps translate content into French, English, or Arabic, with a review by someone fluent in the language.
