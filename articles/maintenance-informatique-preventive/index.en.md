Many businesses only think about IT maintenance when a failure happens. Yet a preventive approach can avoid most incidents and reduce costs in the long run.

## The real cost of a failure

Beyond the repair itself, an IT failure causes a loss of productivity, sometimes data loss, and in some cases damage to your reputation with your clients.

## What preventive maintenance covers

- Regular monitoring of servers and workstations
- Scheduled security updates
- Backup verification
- System cleanup and optimization
- Early replacement of aging hardware

## A contract tailored to your business

The frequency and scope of a maintenance contract should match the size of your business and the criticality of your systems. An SME doesn't have the same needs as a hotel or an accounting firm.
