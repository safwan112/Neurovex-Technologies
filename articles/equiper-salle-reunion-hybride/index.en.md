A hybrid meeting room should let people in the room and remote participants contribute without spending the first ten minutes fixing equipment.

## Start with room size

The number of people and seating layout determine camera coverage, microphone reach, and screen size. A small huddle room needs a different setup from a large table.

## Prioritize audio

Remote participants need to hear every speaker. Test microphones from every seat, then check echo and background noise.

## Make connection simple

Provide a clear way to start a video call, share a screen, and adjust volume. A spare cable and visible instructions prevent common delays.

## Test with your team's tools

Before launch, run a real call using the software your colleagues use. Ask someone unfamiliar with the room to start the meeting; their experience shows what needs simplifying.
