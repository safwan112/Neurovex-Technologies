An accidentally deleted file, a failed computer, or a configuration mistake can stop a team. Backups matter, but the real goal is to recover the right data in time.

## Identify critical data

List client folders, accounting records, databases, and configurations needed for daily work. Decide how long your business can operate without each one.

## Keep separate copies

Store copies on different systems or in different locations. One incident should not affect the originals and every backup at once. Restrict who can delete backup data.

## Test a restore

Regularly restore a sample of files to a test location. Check that they open, how long recovery takes, and who can perform the process.

## Document the recovery plan

Record who starts a restore, where credentials are kept, and which system comes back first. Update the plan whenever your tools change.
