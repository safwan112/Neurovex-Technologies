A business network connects computers, servers, printers, and cloud tools. Poor configuration can turn one failure or unauthorized connection into a disruption for several teams.

## Inventory your equipment

List routers, Wi-Fi access points, switches, and connected devices. Record their location, owner, and last update. This makes forgotten equipment easier to spot.

## Separate different uses

Guest Wi-Fi should not expose internal files. Separate networks for staff, visitors, and shared devices reduce risk and make troubleshooting easier.

## Review access and backups

Change default passwords, enable stronger authentication where possible, and remove access that is no longer needed. Check that essential data is backed up and that a restore has been tested.

## Schedule routine reviews

Updates, Wi-Fi quality, and network alerts deserve regular checks. A short documented review helps teams act before a problem interrupts work.
