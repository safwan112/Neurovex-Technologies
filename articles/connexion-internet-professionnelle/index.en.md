Internet access supports telephony, cloud tools, and customer communication. The right choice depends on actual usage and the cost of an outage.

## How many people work online at once?

Estimate simultaneous video calls, file transfers, and business applications. Check upload speed too: it matters for backups and video meetings.

## What availability do you need?

Ask how incidents are reported and how quickly support responds. A headline speed is of limited value if an outage stops work for hours.

## What is the backup plan?

A second connection, ideally using a different technology, can keep essential tasks running during an outage. Test the switchover in advance.

## Can your internal network keep up?

A fast internet link cannot fix overloaded Wi-Fi or damaged cabling. Test the full path from the provider to your team's devices.
