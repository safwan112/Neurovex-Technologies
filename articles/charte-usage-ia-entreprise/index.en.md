Your employees are probably already using AI tools, sometimes without any framework. A usage policy clarifies what is allowed and protects both the company and its teams.

## List approved tools

State which services can be used, with which accounts, and how to request a new tool. This prevents a spread of unmanaged personal subscriptions.

## Specify prohibited data

Customer personal data, financial information, contracts, access codes: these must not be entered into an unapproved tool. Concrete examples make the rule clearer.

## Require human review

Any AI-generated content intended for a customer, partner, or publication must be reviewed. The user remains responsible for the accuracy of the text, figures, and sources.

## Appoint a point of contact

A named person answers questions, follows new uses, and updates the policy. A review every six months is enough to keep up with the tools.
