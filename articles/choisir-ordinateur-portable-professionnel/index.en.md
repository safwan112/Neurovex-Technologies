A business laptop stays with an employee for several years. Choosing on price alone often leads to slowdowns, breakdowns, and early replacement.

## Match performance to use

Office work and email don't need the same configuration as graphic design or data analysis. Plan for at least 16 GB of memory and SSD storage for comfortable use over several years.

## Consider AI features

Many models now include a chip dedicated to AI. It speeds up features such as noise reduction in video calls or local assistants. Useful for some roles, it isn't essential for everyone.

## Check security and management

Disk encryption, fingerprint or face recognition, and compatibility with remote management tools all make the fleet easier to secure.

## Think warranty and consistency

A three-year on-site warranty and a limited number of models across the company make support and spare parts easier to manage.
