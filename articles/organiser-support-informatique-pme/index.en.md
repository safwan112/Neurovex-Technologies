When several people report problems at once, handling tickets in arrival order is rarely enough. A structured support process protects the most important work.

## Define priority levels

A company-wide outage needs a different response from a software question. Agree in advance on urgency criteria and who should be notified.

## Centralize requests

A single support channel prevents issues from getting lost in calls or messages. Record the user, symptoms, business impact, and steps already tried.

## Track resolution

Show who owns each issue, its current status, and the next step. Keep the affected person informed even when a fix takes time.

## Learn from recurring incidents

If the same problem returns, look for its cause: aging hardware, missed updates, or unclear guidance. A monthly review can turn recurring tickets into preventive action.
