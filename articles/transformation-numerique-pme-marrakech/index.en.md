Digital transformation is no longer reserved for large companies. In Marrakech, more and more SMEs are modernizing their IT, telephony, and office tools to gain efficiency and competitiveness.

## 1. Take stock of where you stand

Before any investment, it's essential to assess your current IT setup: workstations, servers, network, and security level. This audit helps identify priorities and avoid unnecessary spending.

## 2. Make security a priority

IT security should be addressed first: regular backups, network protection, and system updates. A vulnerable infrastructure puts your entire business at risk.

## 3. Modernize gradually

You don't need to change everything at once. A gradual approach - starting with the most critical equipment - limits business disruption and the budget involved.

## 4. Rely on a local partner

Working with a service provider based nearby guarantees a fast response when needed, and a lasting relationship of trust.
