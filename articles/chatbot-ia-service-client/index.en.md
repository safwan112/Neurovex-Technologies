An AI chatbot can answer customers at any hour and take pressure off your team. Without preparation, though, it gives vague or inaccurate answers that hurt your image.

## Define a clear scope

List the most frequent questions: opening hours, lead times, order tracking, pricing, appointments. A chatbot that answers ten topics well beats a tool that answers everything poorly.

## Prepare reliable content

The AI relies on your documents: FAQ, terms and conditions, service sheets. Update them before launch and assign someone to maintain them.

## Plan the human handoff

Customers must be able to reach an agent easily, by phone, email, or WhatsApp. Pass along the conversation history so they don't have to repeat themselves.

## Measure and improve

Track unanswered questions, the handoff rate, and satisfaction. These indicators show where to enrich the content.
