IP telephony is gradually replacing traditional landlines in Moroccan businesses, with lower costs and greater flexibility. Here are the criteria to consider before making your choice.

## Connectivity quality

An IP telephony solution depends entirely on the quality of your internet connection. It's important to assess your available bandwidth before deployment.

## Scalability

Your solution should be able to grow with your team, without requiring a complete infrastructure replacement with every new hire.

## Technical support

A telephony outage can quickly impact your business. Responsive support, ideally local, makes all the difference when an incident occurs.

## Integration with your existing tools

Make sure the solution you choose integrates well with your current business tools (CRM, messaging, etc.).
