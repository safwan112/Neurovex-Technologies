Entering invoices, sorting emails, following up with customers: these repetitive tasks take up a large share of working time. AI and automation tools can lighten part of that load.

## Spot good candidates

A task suits automation when it is frequent, follows stable rules, and relies on digital data. Ask your teams what they redo every week.

## Start small

Choose a single process, such as extracting invoice details into a spreadsheet. Measure the time spent before and after to judge the real gain.

## Keep human validation

At first, have results reviewed. AI can misread a document or misclassify a request. Validation can be reduced once reliability is proven.

## Document how it works

Record which tools are connected, which accounts they use, and who steps in when something fails. An automation nobody understands becomes a risk.
