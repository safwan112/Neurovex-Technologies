Office suites now include AI assistants that can draft a document, summarize a meeting, or build a spreadsheet. Their value depends on how they are introduced in the company.

## Clean up before switching on

An AI assistant draws on the files the user can access. If sharing permissions are too broad, it can surface sensitive documents. Review permissions before the rollout.

## Start with a pilot group

Pick a few users from different departments. Their feedback shows which uses really save time: meeting notes, email replies, spreadsheet formulas, or presentations.

## Teach good practices

A precise request gives a better result. Show how to write a prompt, check the answer, and cite sources. Remind users that the content produced remains their responsibility.

## Assess the cost

These options are often billed per user. Compare the time saved for each role to decide who really needs one.
